## Compatibility and commands

- The [published GPUI 0.2.2 archive](https://static.crates.io/crates/gpui/gpui-0.2.2.crate) has SHA-256 `979b45cfa6ec723b6f42330915a1b3769b930d02b2d505f9697f8ca602bee707`, matching [Cargo.lock](../../../../Cargo.lock). Its [manifest](https://docs.rs/crate/gpui/0.2.2/source/Cargo.toml) declares Apache-2.0 and has no benchmark-support feature. The complete gpui and [gpui-macros 0.2.2](https://docs.rs/crate/gpui-macros/0.2.2/source/src/gpui_macros.rs) sources contain none of the named modern benchmark helpers. They remain upstream references only.
- [src/app.rs](https://docs.rs/crate/gpui/0.2.2/source/src/app.rs) defines `Application::new`, `Application::run`, `App::quit`, `App::spawn` and `App::background_executor`. [src/executor.rs](https://docs.rs/crate/gpui/0.2.2/source/src/executor.rs) defines production foreground/background spawning and `Task`. [src/app/context.rs](https://docs.rs/crate/gpui/0.2.2/source/src/app/context.rs) defines entity-scoped spawning and notification. These are usable production primitives, not a ready-made benchmark runner.
- [Cargo build](https://doc.rust-lang.org/cargo/commands/cargo-build.html) specifies `--locked`, `--release`, `-p`, `--bin` and `--example`; [Cargo targets](https://doc.rust-lang.org/cargo/reference/cargo-targets.html) describes example discovery and dev dependencies. [Workspace](../../../../Cargo.toml), [desktop manifest](../../../../apps/desktop/Cargo.toml) and [toolchain](../../../../rust-toolchain.toml) establish local names, pins and profiles. No probe target is assumed to exist.
- Rust's [`Instant`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/time/struct.Instant.html) provides elapsed wall time; [`black_box`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/hint/fn.black_box.html) is only a best-effort optimization barrier. Neither supplies frame/presentation or process-memory measurement.
- GNU Coreutils [v9.5 `src/timeout.c`](https://github.com/coreutils/coreutils/blob/v9.5/src/timeout.c) defines `--signal`, `--kill-after`, duration suffixes and timeout exit handling. A timeout is failure evidence, not proof of workload completion or orderly teardown.

The standard-library/Cargo manual links describe the retained stable interfaces; they are not claims that the project's pinned toolchain or a probe has been exercised. GPUI archive bytes, rather than its dirty VCS metadata or current Zed main, are the compatibility source. No sample was compiled, no benchmark ran and no performance budget was qualified during this static authoring.
