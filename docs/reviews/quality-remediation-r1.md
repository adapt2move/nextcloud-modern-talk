## Required product integration

The integration author must amend the following existing IDs without changing their scope or renumbering them. These are outstanding cross-document corrections, not edits made in this handoff. Reconcile against the product author's latest revision.

1. **[accounts.md](../product/accounts.md), introduction and `AUTH-003`:** use NC34/35 Login Flow references; restoration requires committed account/generation eligibility plus an accessible confirmed item. An accessible vault item alone is insufficient after unresolved writes, fallback or rejection.
2. **`AUTH-004`:** distinguish known session-only storage from an earlier uncertain provider write. Require durable account-wide restoration suppression before activating a replacement session-only credential, visible pending cleanup/uncertainty, and a repair/cancel outcome if suppression cannot be committed. Add late-completion and crash/restart acceptance; keep fallback an explicit user choice.
3. **`AUTH-005/008`:** preserve older retained credentials during fallback. Permit exact cleanup of the newly attempted generation without calling it deletion of the older account credential; narrow `AUTH-008`'s current “only case” sentence accordingly. Deliberate cancel-and-retain may restore the named older valid session; abandoned generations never activate. Authorized replacement/removal and abandoned-attempt cleanup have different scopes.
4. **`AUTH-007`:** confirmed account loss must invalidate its runtime generation, stop authenticated work and active local capture/publication independently of server leave, suppress automatic reuse and reject obsolete callbacks. Preserve permitted drafts/other accounts; distinguish action denial and conversation/session expiry. New authorization needs fresh checks and deliberate send retry/call rejoin; disclose unconfirmed restart suppression if its metadata write fails.
5. **[calls.md](../product/calls.md), `CALL-006/009/011/012`:** qualify “immediately” relative to observed restrictions and the approved expiry/enforcement bound. Link `Q.SEC-10` for uninterrupted-call freshness, unknown/stale policy, consent/E2EE/grid changes that may lack hash invalidation, and safe publication/subscription suspension. Do not imply instantaneous knowledge of unseen server state or transfer fresh consent to another call.
6. **`CALL-014`:** add confirmed account-loss shutdown and late-callback rejection, plus bounded control/policy-loss handling even when media remains connected. Local stop cannot wait for leave acknowledgement; recovery needs fresh policy/session checks and appropriate user intent. Preserve mute/camera-off state and the existing direct-accept versus voluntary-conference-prejoin distinction. Link `V.AUTH.LOSS` and `V.POLICY.FRESH` acceptance cases.
